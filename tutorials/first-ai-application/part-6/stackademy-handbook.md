# Stackademy Student Handbook

## Enrollment and refunds

You can enroll in a course at any time. Each course starts when you enroll, and you have 16 weeks to finish it.
If a course isn't right for you, you can ask for a full refund within 30 days of enrolling, as long as you have
completed no more than two of its graded assignments. After 30 days, or after your third graded assignment, a
course can't be refunded, but you can transfer your enrollment to another course of the same price or less, once.
To ask for a refund or a transfer, write to the registrar at registrar@stackademy.example.

## Extensions

If you need more time, you can ask your instructor for one extension of up to 4 weeks per course. Ask before your
16 weeks end. A second extension needs the approval of the Dean of Students, and is given only for illness, family
emergencies or military service.

## Grading and certificates

Each course has graded assignments and a final project. You pass a course with an overall grade of 70% or higher.
When you pass, you receive a digital certificate, signed by your instructor, which you can share on LinkedIn. You can
resubmit any graded assignment once, and your higher grade counts.

## The AI Engineering Certificate

Students who pass Artificial Intelligence (CS210), Machine Learning (CS301) and the AI Capstone Project (CS405)
receive the AI Engineering Certificate, a credential of its own, in addition to each course's certificate.

## Accessibility

Every lecture has captions and a transcript. Students who need extra time on graded assignments for a disability
receive it automatically once the accessibility office has confirmed their accommodation. Write to
access@stackademy.example.

## Academic honesty

You may use AI coding assistants on assignments unless an assignment says otherwise, but you must say which ones you
used, and for what, in your submission. Submitting someone else's work as your own fails the assignment the first
time, and the course the second time.
